## 1. Established real-world facts (official sources only)

1. **Exactly two entries per team in Formula 1.** FIA 2026 General Provisions, Article A2.1.3 specifies two cars per F1 team; the Constructors' Championship aggregates both cars' points (A2.1.4). The official F1 guide likewise explains two cars and team scoring. Sources: [FIA Section A PDF](https://www.fia.com/system/files/documents/fia_2026_f1_regulations_-_section_a_general_provisions_-_iss_02_-_2026-02-27.pdf), [F1 Constructors guide](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/the-beginners-guide-to-the-f1-constructors-championship.66nTfWSqrUYv3bnbosPkHV).
2. **Race weekend format.** A standard F1 weekend uses practice, qualifying and a Grand Prix. Sprint weekends add a shorter 100-km Sprint with no mandatory pit stop. Sources: [F1 weekend](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/the-beginners-guide-to-the-formula-1-weekend.5RFZzGXNhEi9AEuMXwo987), [F1 Sprint guide](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/the-beginners-guide-to-the-f1-sprint.55yJBEiF7vYkZEwSV9lZJ9).
3. **Strategic tyres.** Dry tyres are Soft (red, faster-grippier/less durable), Medium (yellow) and Hard (white, more durable), with Intermediate (green) and Full Wet (blue) for rain. Tyre allocation and use are regulated by FIA Section B, Article B6. Sources: [F1 tyre guide](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/the-beginners-guide-to-f1-tyres.61SvF0Kfg29UR2SPhakDqd), [FIA Sporting regulations](https://www.fia.com/regulation/category/110). Do **not** hardcode a universal mandatory number of pit stops; regulations and exceptional events differ. Pit decisions depend on tyre degradation, weather/track condition and race strategy; see [F1 pit-stop anatomy](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/anatomy-of-a-pit-stop-how-do-teams-service-their-cars-in-less-than-two.5p9LNdd8XJdvP4mRsXoGsB).
4. **Car performance is multidimensional.** 2026 active aero trades lower drag/top speed on straights against extra downforce/grip through corners, with energy management significant. Sources: [F1 2026 regulations explainer](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/the-beginners-guide-to-the-2026-regulations.6j0tS0hrHG2T01tpmK6XYz), [F1 aero specialist interview](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/f1-explains-downforce-and-why-f1-cars-have-wings-with-mclaren-aero.10xj8CJPz8s7CmwfoyWmCU).
5. **Useful official measurements exist, but not a complete open three-stat table.** FIA race classifications publish speed-trap km/h by car/driver for a single event (e.g., [2026 Monza Qualifying Speed Trap](https://api.fia.com/events/fia-formula-one-world-championship/season-2026/italian-grand-prix/qualifying-classification)). F1 explains that its telemetry-derived car-performance analysis groups straights and low-/medium-/high-speed corner sections, including corner-exit speeds. Source: [F1 car performance scores](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/rob-smedley-on-car-performance-scores-and-the-data-behind-the-latest-f1.5QoyFi4KL63EULKM4u5mGL). One track's speed trap is **not** a universal car top-speed specification.

## 5. Three racing-card stats — evidence-based scoring protocol (PROPOSED, no invented values)

| Game stat | Acceptable real data source | Proposed transformation | Caveat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Top Speed** | FIA **speed-trap km/h** by same race session and circuit; preferably several comparable tracks | Normalize speed-trap results **within each event** (percentile or rank), then aggregate event-normalized scores; only afterward map to an editable game stat | Affected by slipstream, wing setup, engine deployment, weather and traffic; cannot claim vehicle's physical max speed |
| **Acceleration** | Official longitudinal velocity trace or identical entry/exit speed over measured equal-distance sections | Compare like-for-like speed gain per distance/time across controlled sessions, then normalize | No verified public dataset currently acquired. Mark **DATA_MISSING** rather than invent a stat; FIA speed-trap alone cannot establish acceleration |
| **Handling** | Official F1 telemetry corner-segment performance (low/medium/high speed) or measured corner-only time | Track-normalized corner section performance, keep low-/high-speed handling subdimensions if data permit | Qualifying sector times include straights; cannot be treated as handling without segmentation. If only sector data exist, mark **PROXY_UNVERIFIED** |

**Never copy a published speed-trap table's numbers into the game without checking data-use permission.** Formula 1's own [IP guidelines](https://www.formula1.com/en/information/guidelines.4EOKE9RRqevL4niTK9kWyt) explicitly cover race timing/statistics and official imagery. For development, use the sources to understand racing tradeoffs; for released paid content, legal/licensing review is needed. Mark both the provenance and uncertainty for each score. **No numerical Top Speed/Acceleration/Handling values are proposed in this brief.**

## 6. Game-specific architecture (PROPOSED, owner approval needed)

- **Auto Sim uses exactly 2 active race cars per team** (two independent car/driver pair slots). Each car chooses 1 driver, chassis, tyre and pit plan. Both race results contribute to the **team** total while each driver has their own individual score.
- **Separate motorsport gacha banner/inventory** from the main Avatar / Tactic / Companion banner. Candidate pools: `RACE_DRIVER`, `RACE_CAR`, `RACE_SUPPORT`. **No rates, rarity weights, pity limits, prices, or currencies assigned.**
- **Pre-race:** choose the two racing pairs, adjust optional `aeroBias` (straight-line vs grip), tyre-start compound, planned pit windows, and risk level; weather forecast is uncertainty rather than guaranteed truth.
- **Simulation:** progress sector-by-sector or lap-by-lap; tyre wear, ambient/track wetness, overtaking opportunities, car reliability, racing incidents, safe pit release and pit delay are tunable mechanics. A Sprint mode can omit mandatory stops. Explain player results with event log (e.g. wet tyres at a dry track, undercut/overcut).
- **Championship:** separate `DRIVER_STANDINGS` and `TEAM_STANDINGS`, two-car aggregation; optionally Qualifying + Race + Season as independent modes. Use configurable event scoring instead of assuming every fictional league must copy F1 points exactly.
- **Art and IDs:** original comic race helmets/portraits, brand-new stylized car silhouettes and liveries, original track layouts and venue names. Research/source IDs stay in development metadata, not player-visible marketing.
